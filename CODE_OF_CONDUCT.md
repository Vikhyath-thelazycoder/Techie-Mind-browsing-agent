# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps our community:

- being kind and respectful, including to people who disagree with you
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing someone's private information, such as an address or e-mail, without their explicit
  permission
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when
someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers listed in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md),
through GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is
respected. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code. They follow the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
